# PHAM content principles

- **One idea per paragraph.** Most editorial paragraphs should be 1 to 2 short sentences. Do not repeat the same material, edition count or release claim across adjacent sections.
- **Specific over promotional.** State what the garment is, which stage it is in and what customers need to know. Avoid abstract adjectives, narrative filler and unsupported promises.
- **Punctuation.** Prefer periods, commas and colons. Avoid em dashes in marketing prose unless the punctuation is indispensable. Keep PHAM-001 and numerical ranges intact.
- **Protect facts.** Do not shorten away price, deposit credit, remaining balance, deadline, availability, sample/production status, manufacturing origin, limitation, tax, shipping or refund disclosures.
- **Legal and privacy text is separate.** Never automatically compress policies, agreements, consent or statutory notices.
- **Authoritative data.** Theme copy lives in Liquid sections and template JSON. Shopify Admin product descriptions and SEO are separate resources. Update both sources explicitly and check the storefront.
- **QA.** Check mobile/desktop wrapping, screen-reader labels, all links, toggles and no unexpected horizontal overflow before deployment to the actual Live theme.
